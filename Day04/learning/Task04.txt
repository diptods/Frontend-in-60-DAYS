Que: Why Flexbox Matters?
Ans: 
    Flexbox matters because it revolutionized web layouts, making it incredibly easy to align, space, and distribute items across varying screen sizes without relying on hacky workarounds.
    It provides the foundation for responsive design.
Almost every modern website uses Flexbox.
Que: Why Flexbox is being used?
Ans:
    1. Effortless Alignment and Centering  --justify-content| align-items
    2. Built-in Responsiveness  --flex-grow| flex-shrink| flex-wrap
    3. Order Without Changing HTML
    4. Effortless Spacing  --space-between| space-around

Examples:
[ Navigation bars, Hero sections, Cards, Pricing sections, Dashboards ]

If you understand Flexbox well, your frontend journey becomes much easier.

Flex 
    display: |none|flex|grid|inline|block|contents|inline-flex|inline-grid|inline-table|list-item|run-in|table|table-caption|table-column-group|table-header-group|table-footer-group|table-cell|table-column|table-row|

    Without Flexbox: Boxes stack vertically.
    With Flexbox: Boxes appear horizontally.

Flex Direction
    flex-direction: row|row-reverse|column|column-reverse|initial|inherit;
    flex-flow: flex-direction flex-wrap|initial|inherit;
    flex-basis: number|auto|initial|inherit;
    flex-grow: number|initial|inherit;
    flex-shrink: number|initial|inherit;
    flex-wrap: nowrap|wrap|wrap-reverse|initial|inherit;

float: none|left|right|initial|inherit;

####  Alignment  ####
    align-content: stretch|center|flex-start|flex-end|space-between|space-around|space-evenly|initial|inherit;
    align-self: auto|stretch|center|flex-start|flex-end|baseline|initial|inherit;
    align-items: normal|stretch|positional alignment|flex-start|flex-end|baseline|initial|inherit;

#### justify-content  ####
    justify-content: flex-start|flex-end|center|space-between|space-around|space-evenly|initial|inherit;
    justify-items: legacy|normal|stretch|positional alignment|overflow-alignment|baseline alignment|initial|inherit;
    justify-self: auto|normal|stretch|positional alignment|overflow-alignment|baseline alignment|initial|inherit;

    Most beginners spend too much time choosing colors.
    Professional developers spend more time on:
        spacing
        alignment
        layout
    A website with perfect spacing but simple colors often looks better than a colorful website with poor layout.
